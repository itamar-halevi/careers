# Equal opportunity

Mirelle and our client companies hire on merit against the requirements in
the role page. We do not discriminate on the basis of sex, gender identity,
sexual orientation, age, religion, nationality, ethnicity, disability,
parenthood or pregnancy, reserve-duty status, or political opinion.

Two practical commitments beyond the statement:

1. **Tell us what you need.** Interview adjustments — scheduling around
   reserve duty or childcare, accessible locations, extra time on
   take-homes — are arranged without explanation owed to anyone beyond
   your consultant.
2. **We push back.** When a client states a requirement that proxies for
   discrimination ("young team energy", unnecessary physical requirements),
   we say so and renegotiate it or walk. We have walked.
