# Candidate privacy notice

Short version, in plain language. The Hebrew version (per Israeli
Privacy Protection Law amendment 13) is in this repository at
[docs/privacy-notice-he.md](../docs/privacy-notice-he.md).

**What we collect.** Your CV and anything you send with it, notes from our
conversations, references you approve, and technical correspondence data.

**Why.** To present you to clients for roles you agreed to be presented
for, and — only if you say yes — to keep you in our pool for future roles.

**Who sees it.** The consultant on your search, and the specific client
you approved. We never send your CV to a client you haven't agreed to.
We do not sell data. We do not run candidate data through advertising or
profiling systems.

**How long.** Active processes: until they close. Pool with consent: six
months from last contact, then deleted unless you renew. You can ask us to
delete everything, any time, at mirellepartners@gmail.com — deletion
is confirmed in writing within 7 days.

**Your rights.** Access, correction, deletion, and withdrawal of consent,
under the Privacy Protection Law. Our data practices contact is Tomer R.
at the same address.
