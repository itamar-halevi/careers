# Interview feedback - template

Fill within 24 hours of the interview. Send to the owning consultant;
do not forward raw notes to the client.

**Candidate:** [initials only, e.g. "A.B."]  **Role:** [role title]
**Interviewer:** [name]  **Date:** [date]  **Stage:** [intro / manager / practical / final]

## Against the brief

For each must-have from the role page: evidence heard, evidence missing.
Quote the candidate, not your impression of the quote.

## Signal summary

- Technical depth: [strong / adequate / thin] - one line why
- Communication: [strong / adequate / thin] - one line why
- Ownership: [strong / adequate / thin] - one line why

## Recommendation

[advance / hold / no], with the single strongest reason. A "no" must name
what would have changed it - we give this to the candidate.
