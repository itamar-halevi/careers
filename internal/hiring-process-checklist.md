# Hiring process checklist - per role

Owner: the consultant who opened the search. Tick in order; do not skip
the answer steps - they are the whole brand.

## Opening

- [ ] Client brief filled and salary range confirmed in writing
- [ ] Role page in /roles with range, location, must-haves
- [ ] Site list updated (/docs/roles copy + index line)

## Running

- [ ] Every application answered: confirmation in 3 business days,
      yes/no within a week
- [ ] Scorecard per candidate per stage
- [ ] Practical stage capped at 3 hours; final-stage work is paid
- [ ] No CV sent to any client without the candidate's explicit yes

## Closing

- [ ] Offer/rejection with reason to every finalist
- [ ] Pool consent asked explicitly; delete the rest per privacy notice
- [ ] Role moved to /roles/filled with the filled banner
- [ ] Site list updated
