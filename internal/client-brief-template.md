# Client brief - template

Fill on the kickoff call, before we write the role page. If a field is
empty, the role does not go up.

**Client:** [codename until the candidate agrees to be presented]
**Contact:** [name, role]  **Date:** [date]

## Must confirm before we publish

- Salary range we may advertise (required - we do not post without it)
- Location and on-site expectation, honestly stated
- The three must-haves, in the client's own words
- Who interviews at each stage, and how long each stage takes
- What "no" feedback we may pass to candidates

## Good to have

- Team size and reporting line
- Why the last person left / why the seat is new
- What a great first 90 days looks like
