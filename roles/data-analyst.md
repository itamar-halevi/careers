# Data Analyst

**Client:** e-commerce group (~200 people), three consumer brands.
**Location:** Haifa, hybrid (2 days on site). **Type:** Full-time.
**Salary range:** 20,000–27,000 ILS gross/month.

## The role

Third analyst on the central data team, supporting the retention and CRM
marketers. The warehouse is BigQuery with dbt; dashboards in Looker. The
team's bottleneck is not SQL — it is turning marketing questions into
answerable ones, so this role is closer to the business than most analyst
seats at this level.

## What we're looking for

- 2+ years in an analytics role with production SQL (any warehouse).
- dbt or equivalent transformation-layer experience.
- You have explained a cohort analysis to a non-analyst in the last year
  and can tell us how it went.
- Hebrew required — the stakeholders you will support work in Hebrew.

## Nice to have

- Python for analysis (pandas is plenty).
- Marketing measurement exposure: MMM, incrementality tests, attribution
  arguments you've had.
