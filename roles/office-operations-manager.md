# Office & Operations Manager — Mirelle (internal)

**Employer:** Mirelle Talent Partners (this is our own seat, not a client
role). **Location:** Tel Aviv area - meetings by appointment.
**Type:** Part-time,
60% position — three full days a week, days flexible.
**Salary:** 9,000–11,000 ILS gross/month (pro-rated from full-time).

## The role

Three of us recruit; Tomer runs contracts, invoicing, and our tooling.
The rest of the day-to-day needs an owner. You would own: vendor
relationships, scheduling and meeting logistics, travel coordination, and
keeping our candidate-facing templates and materials current. Tomer stays on the
contracts and systems side - the two of you will overlap plenty, and that
is the point.

This role talks to candidates a lot — scheduling, expense reimbursements,
and the small kindnesses of a hiring process. It needs someone who
understands that a candidate waiting for a schedule is a person having a
stressful week.

## What we're looking for

- 2+ years office management, operations, or executive administration,
  ideally in a small company where the role meant "everything".
- Fluent Hebrew and working English.
- Spreadsheet comfort. We run on Google Workspace and Notion.

## Nice to have

- Bookkeeping software exposure (Hashavshevet/ Priority a plus).
- Previous HR-adjacent work.
