# Product Designer

**Client:** health-tech startup (~35 people) building a patient-facing
chronic-care app. **Location:** Remote, EU/IL time zones; quarterly meetups
in Tel Aviv. **Type:** Full-time. **Salary range:** 26,000–34,000 ILS
gross/month or contractor equivalent.

## The role

Second designer, reporting to the Head of Product. The first designer owns
the clinician dashboard; you would own the patient app end to end —
onboarding, medication tracking, and the messaging surface between patients
and care teams.

This is a research-heavy seat. The client runs biweekly patient interviews
and expects the designer in them.

## What we're looking for

- 3+ years designing shipped mobile products (iOS/Android, not only web).
- A portfolio that shows process, not just screens: what you tried, what
  failed, what changed.
- Comfort with regulated or sensitive domains. Prior health/finance work
  helps; care about the subject matters more.
- Hebrew and English reading proficiency — the research corpus is both.

## Nice to have

- Accessibility certification or demonstrable WCAG 2.2 work.
- Motion design for micro-interactions.
