# Our interview process

Every search runs the same shape. Timelines below are commitments, not
aspirations — the consultant owning the search is measured on them.

## 1. Intro call — 30 minutes, with us

Usually Itamar, your candidate-experience consultant. What the role actually is (including the client name),
what you are looking for, salary alignment, logistics. You will know at
the end of this call whether we are moving forward, or within 48 hours
after.

## 2. Client interview one — 60 minutes

With your direct manager-to-be. No live coding, no whiteboard puzzles.
Expect questions about your actual past work: decisions you made, what
broke, what you would do differently.

## 3. Practical stage

Format depends on the role — a small take-home, a portfolio walkthrough,
or a working session. Two rules we hold clients to: it never exceeds three
hours of your time, and if you reach the final stage it is paid.

## 4. Final stage — half day

Meet the team, meet a stakeholder or two, ask them anything. For on-site
roles this happens on site. We reimburse travel.

## 5. References and offer

Two references, ten-minute calls, we handle scheduling. Offer within three
business days of the final stage. We will tell you the real deadline the
client gave us — no invented urgency.

## If it's a no

You hear it from a person, with the actual reason at the level of "which
part of the loop decided it". We keep your file (with your permission) and
call you when something fits — being told no once is
not a verdict on you, and we mean that.
