# What "benefits" mean at our clients

Candidates ask us what to compare beyond salary. Our standard notes:

## The baseline you should expect in Israeli tech (2026)

- Pension (keren pensia) with employer contribution, and keren hishtalmut
  (study fund) — standard, usually from day one; if a client offers it
  only after a year, we will flag it.
- 18–22 vacation days; 10bis/Cibus lunch card or equivalent.
- Sick days from day one by law; the difference is whether the client pays
  100% from the first day or follows the statutory ramp.

## What actually differentiates offers

- **Equity terms**, not equity headlines. Ask about strike price, vesting
  cliffs, and what happens on acquisition. We will help you read the
  paperwork — that's part of what we do.
- **Real hybrid policy.** "Hybrid" can mean one WFH day or four. Our role
  pages state the actual days when the client let us.
- **On-call compensation.** If the role has on-call, it should be paid.
  Every engineering role we list states its arrangement.

## Negotiation

Tell us your range early and honestly and we will hold it. Real numbers
get real offers; lowball anchors get negotiations nobody enjoys.
