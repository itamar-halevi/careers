# How to apply

## For a listed role

1. Read the full role page in [the roles list](./index.html#roles). Check the location and
   the "what we're looking for" section honestly — we would rather get
   fewer, better applications.
2. Send your CV to **itamar1@tutamail.com** with the role's
   subject line as listed on the role page.
3. Attach or link anything the role page asks for (portfolio for design,
   for example). A cover letter is optional everywhere; if you write one,
   [our guide](cover-letter-guide.md) explains what we read it for.

## Open application

No fitting role? Same address, subject "Open application". Tell us what
you want to do next and what you are good at, in plain language. If you tell us to, we keep
your application for six months and search it first when a new search
opens - just write "keep me in the pool" in your email. Otherwise we
delete it when the process ends, per our [privacy notice](./privacy-notice.md).

## What happens next

- **Within 3 business days:** confirmation from a person (usually Itamar),
  not an autoresponder.
- **Within a week:** a yes/no on moving to a first call, with a
  reason if no.

If we miss a deadline, nudge us. It means we are behind, not that you were
forgotten — and the nudge genuinely helps.

## What we read first

In order: your most recent role and what you shipped there; the shape of
your trajectory; then everything else. A one-page CV is fine. Five pages
of everything-you-have-ever-done is not an advantage.
