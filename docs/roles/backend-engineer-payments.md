# Backend Engineer — Payments Platform

**Client:** Series B fintech (~90 people), licensed EMI, processing B2B
cross-border payments. **Location:** Tel Aviv, hybrid (3 days on site).
**Type:** Full-time. **Salary range:** 32,000–42,000 ILS gross/month,
depending on seniority, plus equity.

## The team

Nine engineers, split into ledger, integrations, and platform. You would
join the ledger team: double-entry core, reconciliation, and the nightly
settlement pipeline. Stack: Kotlin, PostgreSQL, Kafka, Kubernetes on GCP.

## What you would own

- The posting pipeline between the ledger core and three banking partners.
- Reconciliation tooling — the team is mid-way through replacing a
  spreadsheet-driven process with an automated matcher.
- On-call for the ledger service, one week in five, paid.

## What we're looking for

- 4+ years backend engineering, at least two in a system where correctness
  of stored data mattered (finance, billing, logistics, inventory).
- Solid SQL beyond CRUD: window functions, query plans, indexing tradeoffs.
- Experience with at least one message broker in production.
- You can read a balance-sheet-adjacent spec without panicking. No finance
  background needed, but you should be curious about it.

## Nice to have

- Kotlin or JVM depth; otherwise Java/Go/C# transfers fine.
- ISO 20022 or payment-scheme exposure.
- Hebrew is not required; the codebase and docs are English.

Intro call (Itamar, 30 min) → technical conversation with the team lead

the final stage) → final half-day with the team. Full details in
