# CV template

You do not have to use this template — any readable CV works. This is just
what our consultants find fastest to read, and what we send back when
someone asks "how should my CV look".

---

## [Full name]
[City] · [phone] · [email] · [LinkedIn / portfolio / GitHub if relevant]

**Summary** — Two or three lines. What you do, how long, what you want
next. Skip adjectives; we will form our own.

### Experience

**[Title] — [Company], [dates]**
One line on what the company does if it is not obvious. Then 3–5 bullets
of what *you* did, each with a number where one exists: scale, latency,
revenue, team size, error rates.

(Repeat, most recent first. Roles older than ten years can be one line.)

### Education & training
Degree, institution, year. Courses only if they changed what you can do.

### Skills
Tools and technologies you could work in tomorrow without a ramp-up week.
Separate "work in daily" from "have used".

### Languages
With honest levels.

---

## Notes we give candidates

- Do not list every technology you have ever touched. A long skills list
  reads as its opposite.
- Gaps are fine. A one-line note ("2022: family care") is plenty; you owe
  nobody the story.
- PDF, not Word. Name it `Firstname-Lastname-CV.pdf` — our files thank you.
