# Cover letters — what we actually use them for

We do not require cover letters, and a missing one is never the reason for
a no. When you do write one, here is what it does for you:

**It explains the non-obvious.** A career change, a relocation, a gap, a
role you're applying to sideways. The CV shows what happened; the letter
is where the "why" lives.

**It shows us the specific role, not the boilerplate.** Two sentences that
reference something real about the role or the client tell us you read the
ad. A letter that could have gone to fifty companies tells us that too.

**It sets tone for a people business.** We place people. Write like one.

## Keep it to

Three short paragraphs, under 200 words: why this role, why you, anything
the CV can't say. That's all.
