# About Mirelle

Mirelle Talent Partners was founded in early 2026 by Mirelle Azoulay,
after twelve years recruiting in-house for Israeli tech. We are brand new
and still building our presence. We run hiring out of this repository -
our internal careers desk - while the public website is under
construction, and we share it openly with candidates.

Mirelle kept seeing the same failure from both sides: companies
drowning in CVs they never read, and strong candidates filtered out by
keyword matching before a person ever looked at them.

We are small on purpose. Four people, no account managers, no resume
forwarding. Every candidate we put in front of a client has passed a real
conversation with the person who owns that search.

## What we recruit for

- Engineering (backend, frontend, mobile, QA automation)
- Data (analysts, analytics engineers)
- Product and design
- Operations and finance staff for our clients' back offices

## What we don't do

- Volume staffing or temp agency work
- Executive search above VP level
- Roles outside Israel and European time zones

## How we work with clients

Retained or success-fee, never both on the same search. We cap ourselves at
six active searches so each one gets real hours. If we don't believe we can
fill a role, we say so in the first call and decline it.
